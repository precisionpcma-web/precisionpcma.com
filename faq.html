Payment and pricing

How do I pay?

Cash, card (tap to pay) or bank transfer. Repairs are paid when you get your machine back, along with any travel fee. Bank transfers go through a secure Stripe invoice we text or email you, and take a few business days to clear. If we order the parts for a custom build, the parts and the $25 handling fee are paid before we order anything. We don't take Venmo or Zelle, and we'll never ask for gift cards.

What if my repair needs parts?

Parts aren't included in labor prices. We'll add them to your quote, and we may ask you to pay for them before we order. The job takes a little longer while they ship.

Do I sign anything?

Yes. You sign a short service agreement at pickup, or before we order anything for a new build. It covers the price you approved, your data and our policies.

Pickup and travel

How far do you travel?

Anywhere in Franklin County. Pickup is free in Greenfield and starts at $15 elsewhere, agreed before pickup. Farther than that, just ask.

What should I have ready at pickup?

Your charger and anything else that goes with it, a backup of anything important if the computer still turns on, and your login if we'll need it to test. You give us your login in person, and we never store it.

What if I don't go ahead after the diagnosis?

That's fine. The diagnosis is free and you'll get your machine back as is. If you're outside Greenfield, the travel fee we agreed on still applies, since it covers the trip.

Do you work on Macs?

No, we only work on Windows desktops and laptops, plus custom PC builds.

Thermal and tuning

Why does a laptop repaste cost more?

On most gaming laptops, the CPU and GPU share one heatsink that also cools the VRMs and VRAM. Those chips sit at a different height, so thermal putty fills the gap between them and the heatsink. Lifting the heatsink tears the putty, so it always has to be replaced too. The putty and the extra work are included in the price.

Why use thermal putty instead of pads?

Putty squishes to fill uneven gaps, so it keeps full contact with the heatsink where a pad of the wrong thickness can leave an air gap or keep the cooler from seating properly. That's why many gaming laptops and some newer GPUs ship with putty from the factory.

My laptop came with liquid metal. Can you service it?

Yes. We fully clean off the liquid metal and switch to PTM7950, which runs nearly as cool without the risk of the metal pumping out or shorting nearby parts. That's our $125 laptop PTM7950 service.

Is overclocking safe?

We stay within safe voltages and stability-test every change before your machine comes back. Results depend on your hardware, no specific gain is guaranteed, and some manufacturers say overclocking can void their warranty, so we'll go over what to expect before starting.

Custom builds

What's lifetime build support?

Every custom build includes free troubleshooting and diagnosis, help with manufacturer warranty claims (RMAs), a yearly dust-out, and driver and BIOS updates for as long as the original owner has the PC. Replacement parts aren't included unless the manufacturer's warranty covers them, and the travel fee still applies outside Greenfield.

Can I cancel a custom build?

Yes. If you ordered the parts, you own them, so you can cancel any time before we start building at no charge. If we ordered them for you, the parts are yours and their cost and the $25 handling fee aren't refundable. You can take the parts, or we'll try to return them and pass along whatever the store refunds, minus restocking fees and return shipping.

Policies and privacy

The short version. The full terms are in the service agreement you sign at pickup.

Estimates and approval

We'll give you a price before starting, and nothing happens without your OK. If we find something unexpected, we'll let you know right away. No fix, no fee, and repairs are covered for 30 days after your machine comes back.

Back up your data

Backing up your files before pickup is your responsibility. We're careful, but data can be lost, especially on failing hardware, and we can't be responsible for it.

Your device, files and messages

We only access your computer to do the work you approved. We never share or keep your files, and only copy them if you ask for a data transfer. Messages from our forms go through a third-party form service to our email, are only used to reply to you, and are never sold.

Return window

We'll let you know when your machine is ready. If it isn't picked up or accepted back within 90 days of that, it's considered abandoned and may be recycled or sold to cover costs. We'll try to reach you before that happens.
