Payment and pricing

How do I pay?

Cash, check, card tap or bank transfer. For now, please make checks out to Ben Wheeler. The service call is paid at the visit. Bench repairs are paid in full when you get your machine back; we don't do payment plans. If a check is returned, you still owe the amount plus the bank's returned-check fee. For a bank transfer, we text or email you a secure Stripe invoice. It takes up to 4 business days to clear, and we return your machine once it does, so pay cash or tap for same-day return. If we order the parts for a custom build, the whole build (parts, the $25 handling fee, labor and add-ons) is paid upfront by cash, check or bank transfer before we order anything. We don't take Venmo or Zelle, and we'll never ask for gift cards.

How does hourly pricing work?

Jobs that aren't on our price list are $75 an hour at home, with a 1-hour minimum, then 30-minute steps. Setup jobs at your place, like Wi-Fi or a printer, are hourly from the start. Business work is always $100 an hour. You get an estimate first, and we check with you before going over it.

What if my repair needs parts?

Parts aren't included in labor prices. We'll add them to your quote. Parts under $100 are paid when you get your machine back. For bigger orders, we ask you to pay for them by cash, check or bank transfer before we order. If you pay by check, we order once it clears. The job takes a little longer while they ship.

What does the 30-day guarantee cover?

Every job we do, for 30 days from when the work is finished (or from when your machine comes back, for bench work). If the same problem comes back, we fix it again for free, trip included. It doesn't cover anything that happens afterward, like a drop, a spill, or your own overclocking or other changes, or an unrelated problem. A new part that fails is covered by its maker's warranty, and we'll help you with the claim.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

Tell us within 30 days and we'll recheck it or redo it for free. We don't give cash refunds for work done as agreed.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes, as long as your business doesn't already have someone handling its IT, whether an employee or an outside company. Business work is $100 an hour with a 1-hour minimum, then billed in 30-minute increments after that, plus the service call. If your business has more than one location, each one we drive to is its own service call. It covers on-site jobs, multiple machines and upgrade planning. All business work is billed hourly, including jobs that have a flat price for homes. Home pricing applies unless it's a workplace with employees or several work computers, and we'll confirm which one applies before any work starts.

Can you help me pick a laptop?

Yes. Quick buying questions are always free. Tell us what you'll use it for and your budget, and we'll point you in the right direction. If you want an in-depth comparison, we'll tell you what that costs before we start. We may suggest a Mac if it fits you best, but our setup and repairs are Windows only.

Do I sign anything?

For a quick fix, no. You OK the price before we start, and you get an itemized receipt. Our terms are always on our website. If your computer goes to our bench, you sign our service agreement first, every time. For a new build, you sign it before we order anything. The agreement covers the price you approved, your data and our policies.

Yes. Advice by phone or text is always free, and so is figuring out what's wrong and giving you a price. What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Just let us know, any time, and there's no charge. We'll confirm your appointment the day before. If no one's home 15 minutes after the appointment time (or after we arrive, if we're running late) and you didn't let us know, that's a no-show, and the $45 service call may apply. Your charger and any cables or accessories, a backup of your important files if the computer still turns on, and your login if we need it to test. You give us your login in person, and we never store it. For a password reset, we'll ask for proof that the computer is yours.

What if I don't go ahead after the diagnosis?

That's fine. The diagnosis is free. We put your computer back together the way we got it and return it at no extra charge, usually within a week. The service call you already paid, if your town has one, covers the trip back.

Thermal and tuning

Why does laptop thermal service cost more than a desktop?

A desktop processor just needs the cooler taken off and fresh paste put on. On most gaming laptops, the same cooler also sits on smaller memory and power chips, with soft pads or putty filling the gap. Those tear when the cooler comes off, so we always replace them. We use a long-lasting thermal pad on the main chips and thermal putty on the smaller ones. The extra work is included in the $125 price. We don't do thermal service on desktop graphics cards.

Why use thermal putty on the smaller chips?

Putty squishes to fill uneven gaps, so it stays in full contact with the cooler. A standard pad of the wrong thickness can leave an air gap or stop the cooler from sitting flat. That's why many gaming laptops and some newer graphics cards come with putty from the factory.

My laptop came with liquid metal. Can you service it?

Yes. Some laptops use liquid metal instead of paste to move heat. We clean it all off and switch to a long-lasting thermal pad. It usually runs a few degrees warmer than fresh liquid metal, but it can't spread onto nearby parts or dry out over time. It's included in our $125 laptop thermal service at no extra charge. If the liquid metal has already damaged something, we'll show you and quote the repair first.

Is overclocking safe?

Advanced tuning is for desktops. We keep voltages and temperatures within careful limits for your hardware and test every change for stability before your machine comes back. Results depend on your hardware, and no specific gain is guaranteed. AMD and Intel say overclocking, including turning on memory speed profiles (XMP or EXPO), can void their warranties. Some graphics card makers say the same. We'll go over what to expect before we start.

Custom builds

Can I use parts I already have?

Yes. List them on the build form, with model numbers if you know them. We'll check they're compatible and plan the build around them. Older parts get tested before we build, and if one isn't up to the job, we'll tell you before anything is ordered. If a part you buy doesn't match the part list we gave you and we need an extra trip, that trip is a new service call (free in Greenfield).

How long does a build sheet price last?

7 days. Part prices change, so if the total goes up more than $25 before we order, we'll check with you first.

My teen is planning the build. Who needs to sign?

A parent or guardian. Teens are welcome to plan the build with us, but a parent approves the build sheet, signs the agreement and pays.

What's lifetime build support?

Every custom build includes free troubleshooting and diagnosis, help with manufacturer warranty claims (RMAs), a yearly dust-out, and driver and BIOS updates for as long as the original owner has the PC. Replacement parts aren't included unless the manufacturer's warranty covers them. Visits cost the usual service call for your town. Builds delivered before October 12, 2026, keep free visits in Greenfield.

Can I cancel a custom build?

Yes. If you ordered the parts, you own them, so you can cancel at no charge any time before we start building. If we ordered them for you, the parts are still yours, but their cost and the $25 handling fee aren't refundable. Labor and add-ons are refunded if we haven't started building. You can take the parts, or we'll try to return them and pass along whatever the store refunds, minus restocking fees and return shipping.

Policies and privacy

The short version. The full terms are in our service agreement (PDF), which you sign before your computer comes to our bench.

Estimates and approval

We'll give you a price before starting, and nothing happens without your OK. If we find something unexpected, we'll let you know right away. No fix, no repair fee (the service call still applies). Every job has a 30-day guarantee: if the same problem comes back, we fix it again for free.

Back up your data

Backing up your files before the visit is your responsibility. We're careful, but data can be lost, especially on failing hardware, and we can't be responsible for it.

Your device, files and messages

We only access your computer to do the work you approved. We never share or keep your files. We only copy them when the job needs it, like a data transfer or saving files from a failing drive, and delete those copies afterward. Messages from our forms go through a third-party form service to our email, are only used to reply to you, and are never sold.

Return window

We'll let you know when your machine is ready to come back. If we can't arrange a return within 90 days of that, it's considered abandoned and may be recycled or sold to cover costs, with the drive wiped first. We'll try to reach you before that happens.
