Thermal service

Overheating and loud fans

Loud fans, slowing down when hot, or shutting off during heavy use. Heat isn't always the cause, so we start with the free diagnosis. Thermal work is done at our bench and includes a dust-out.

MachinePriceat our bench
Desktop processor (CPU)We take off the cooler, clean it and put fresh thermal paste on the processor$60
Desktop graphics card (GPU)A long-lasting thermal pad on the main chip, plus fresh thermal putty on the smaller memory and power chips that share its cooler$125
LaptopA long-lasting thermal pad on the processor and graphics chip, plus fresh thermal putty on the smaller memory and power chips where your model has them$125
Why graphics cards and laptops get thermal pads and putty

Ordinary thermal paste dries out over time. On graphics cards and laptops we use a long-lasting thermal pad that melts into place when it warms up and lasts much longer than paste. On graphics cards and most gaming laptops, smaller memory and power chips sit under the same cooler on soft pads or putty. Those tear or lose contact when the cooler comes off, so we replace them with fresh thermal putty. Laptops that came with liquid metal are cleaned and switched to a thermal pad at no extra charge. We warm every one up a few times so it settles before your machine comes back. We check your model first. Cards with a warranty seal or unusual pads get a quote.
