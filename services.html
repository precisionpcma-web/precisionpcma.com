Thermal service

Runs hot or loud

Loud fans, thermal throttling or shutdowns under load. We clean off the old compound and replace it, and every machine gets a full dust-out.

Why laptops cost more

On most gaming laptops, one heatsink covers the CPU, GPU, VRMs and VRAM. Lifting it tears the thermal putty on the VRMs and VRAM, so we always replace it with Upsiren UTP-8. The putty and the extra teardown are included. Liquid metal laptops are cleaned and switched to PTM7950.
