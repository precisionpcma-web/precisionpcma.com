Services

Windows desktops, laptops and custom builds. We don't work on Macs. If something's outside what we do, we'll tell you right away.

Repair and troubleshooting

Won't turn on, keeps crashing, blue screens, or just painfully slow. We track down what's actually causing it and fix that.

Custom PC builds

Gaming, work or budget builds. We pick parts that fit your budget, put it together, tidy the cables and test it. The parts list is free.

Thermal repaste

Running hot or slowing down under load? Desktops get fresh paste on the CPU and new pads on the GPU. Laptops get the CPU, GPU and VRM putty redone for one flat price.

Tune-ups and virus removal

Slow startup, pop-ups or a computer acting weird. We clean it up, remove malware and set up protection so it stays that way.

Data transfer and setup

Getting a new computer? We'll move your files over and set it up how you like it. Billed hourly, with an estimate first.

Not sure what's wrong?

Just describe it in the form. The diagnosis is free, and we'll lay out your options before doing anything.

How it works

You don't have to go anywhere. We pick your computer up and bring it back.

  1. Send a request

    Fill out the form and tell us what's going on. We'll get back to you within 24 hours.

  2. Pickup

    We'll set a time to grab it from your place or a public spot that works for you. Outside Franklin County? Just ask.

  3. Diagnosis and quote

    We figure out what's wrong for free, then send you a price. Nothing happens until you say yes.

  4. Return

    Most jobs take 1–3 days, longer if we need to order parts. We clean it, test it and bring it back.

About Precision PC

Precision PC is solely owned and operated by Ben Wheeler. He's been fixing computers since he was a kid and building them for people and local businesses since 2012.

We don't have a storefront, which keeps prices down, and you deal with the same person start to finish. Before you pay, you'll know exactly what was wrong and what we did about it.

Common questions

Quick answers to what people usually ask.

How do I pay?

Cash or Venmo, when you get your machine back.

Do you work on Macs?

No, we only work on Windows desktops and laptops, plus custom PC builds.

What if I don't go ahead after the diagnosis?

That's fine. The diagnosis is free and you'll get your machine back as is. If you're outside Greenfield, the travel fee we agreed on still applies, since it covers the trip.

What should I have ready at pickup?

Your charger and anything else that goes with it, a backup of anything important, and your login if we'll need it to test. You give us your login in person, and we never store it.

What if parts need to be ordered?

Parts are included in your quote. For special-order parts we may ask for a deposit first, and the job takes a little longer while they ship.

How far do you travel?

Anywhere in Franklin County, and pickup is free in Greenfield. Farther than that, just ask.

Policies and privacy

The short version. The full terms are on the service agreement you'll sign at pickup.

Estimates and approval

We'll give you a price before starting, and nothing happens without your OK. If we find something unexpected, we'll let you know right away.

Back up your data

Backing up your files before pickup is your responsibility. We're careful, but data can be lost, especially on failing hardware, and we can't be responsible for it.

Your device, files and messages

We only access your computer to do the work you approved. We never share or keep your files, and only copy them if you ask for a data transfer. Messages from the form go through a third-party form service to our email, are only used to reply to you, and are never sold.

Return window

We'll let you know when your machine is ready. If it isn't picked up or accepted back within 90 days of that, it's considered abandoned and may be recycled or sold to cover costs. We'll try to reach you before that happens.
