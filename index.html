Maintenance & Other
Popular

Thermal repaste

PC or laptop running hot and throttling? Fresh paste can drop temps significantly. Laptop repastes cover the CPU, GPU, and VRM thermal putty replacement in one price, no add-ons. Desktop GPU repastes include new thermal pads.

Dust removal & cleaning

Full disassembly, compressed air blowout of fans, heatsinks, vents, and filters. Pairs well with a thermal repaste for maximum cooling improvement.

Virus & malware removal

Infected, hijacked, or just acting strange? Full cleanup and protection setup so it doesn't happen again.
