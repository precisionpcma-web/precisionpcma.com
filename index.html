Services

Windows desktops, laptops and custom builds. We don't work on Macs. If something's outside what we do, we'll tell you right away.

Repair and troubleshooting

No power, no POST, boot loops, BSODs, freezing or random shutdowns. We isolate the faulty part, driver or setting and fix the root cause, including part swaps like storage, RAM, power supplies and laptop batteries. We don't do board-level or soldering repairs.

Custom PC builds

Gaming, workstation or budget builds. Answer a few questions in the build planner and we'll send you a build sheet with every part, its price and live price links. Order the parts yourself, or have us order them for you, and we build it with clean cable management, the latest stable BIOS, Windows and drivers, XMP or EXPO and a full stress test. Every build comes with lifetime build support.

Performance tuning

Manual RAM timing tuning, CPU and GPU overclocking or undervolting, PBO and Curve Optimizer on AMD, power limits and undervolting on Intel, BIOS setup and custom fan curves. Every change is stability-tested before your machine comes back. Laptops are limited to what their BIOS allows, usually a GPU undervolt plus fan and power profiles.

Thermal service

Running hot or thermal throttling? We clean off the old thermal compound and apply high-end paste or PTM7950 to the CPU and GPU, and replace the thermal pads or Upsiren UTP-8 putty that fills the gap between the VRMs, VRAM and the heatsink.

Windows cleanup and malware removal

Startup bloat, background junk, pop-ups and malware. We clean up Windows, remove the malware, update drivers and set up protection to help keep it that way.

Data transfer and setup

Getting a new computer? We'll move your files over and set it up how you like it. Billed hourly, with an estimate first.

Buying and upgrade advice

Not sure which laptop or desktop to buy, or whether an upgrade is worth it for your work machine? Send us your budget and what you use it for, and we'll give you a straight recommendation for free. For a full written plan comparing specific laptops or prebuilts, it's $40, and that comes off the bill if we set it up for you. Parts lists for custom builds are always free. No pickup needed.

Common questions

Quick answers to what people usually ask.

How do I pay?

Cash or Venmo, when you get your machine back. For custom builds, you either order the parts yourself, or we order them for you, with the parts and a $25 handling fee paid upfront by cash or Zelle.

What's lifetime build support?

Every custom build includes free troubleshooting and diagnosis, help with manufacturer warranty claims (RMAs), a yearly dust-out, and driver and BIOS updates for as long as you own the PC. Replacement parts aren't included unless the manufacturer's warranty covers them, and the travel fee still applies outside Greenfield.

Can I cancel a custom build?

Yes. If you ordered the parts, you own them, so you can cancel any time before we start building at no charge. If we ordered them for you, we return what we can and refund what the stores refund, minus restocking fees and return shipping. The $25 handling fee isn't refunded once parts are ordered.

Do you work on Macs?

No, we only work on Windows desktops and laptops, plus custom PC builds.

Why does a laptop repaste cost more?

On most gaming laptops, the CPU and GPU share one heatsink that also cools the VRMs and VRAM. Those chips sit at a different height than the CPU and GPU, so thermal putty fills the gap between them and the heatsink. Lifting the heatsink to repaste tears the putty, so it always has to be replaced too. The putty and the extra work are included in the price.

Why use thermal putty instead of pads?

Putty squishes to fill uneven gaps, so it keeps full contact with the heatsink where a pad of the wrong thickness can leave an air gap or keep the cooler from seating properly. That's why many gaming laptops and some newer GPUs ship with putty from the factory.

My laptop came with liquid metal. Can you service it?

Yes. We fully clean off the liquid metal and switch to PTM7950, which runs nearly as cool without the risk of the metal pumping out or shorting nearby parts over time.

Is overclocking safe?

We stay within safe voltages and stability-test every change before your machine comes back. Results depend on your hardware, and some manufacturers say overclocking can void their warranty, so we'll go over what to expect before starting.

What if I don't go ahead after the diagnosis?

That's fine. The diagnosis is free and you'll get your machine back as is. If you're outside Greenfield, the travel fee we agreed on still applies, since it covers the trip.

What should I have ready at pickup?

Your charger and anything else that goes with it, a backup of anything important if the computer still turns on, and your login if we'll need it to test. You give us your login in person, and we never store it.

What if parts need to be ordered?

Parts are included in your quote. For special-order parts we may ask for a deposit first, and the job takes a little longer while they ship.

How far do you travel?

Anywhere in Franklin County, and pickup is free in Greenfield. Farther than that, just ask.

Policies and privacy

The short version. The full terms are on the service agreement you'll sign at pickup.

Estimates and approval

We'll give you a price before starting, and nothing happens without your OK. If we find something unexpected, we'll let you know right away.

Back up your data

Backing up your files before pickup is your responsibility. We're careful, but data can be lost, especially on failing hardware, and we can't be responsible for it.

Your device, files and messages

We only access your computer to do the work you approved. We never share or keep your files, and only copy them if you ask for a data transfer. Messages from the form go through a third-party form service to our email, are only used to reply to you, and are never sold.

Return window

We'll let you know when your machine is ready. If it isn't picked up or accepted back within 90 days of that, it's considered abandoned and may be recycled or sold to cover costs. We'll try to reach you before that happens.
