Popular

Thermal repaste

Policies

Policies & privacy

Plain-English expectations, so there are no surprises.

Estimates & approval

You're quoted before any work starts, and nothing proceeds without your approval. Parts are extra unless stated. No fix, no fee. Anything unexpected is communicated to you promptly.

Back up your data

Please back up important files before pickup. Every reasonable precaution is taken, but data loss can happen, especially with failing hardware, and Precision PC can't be responsible for lost data.

Your device & files

Your device is accessed only to complete the requested work. Your files are not copied, shared, or kept.

Return window

You'll be notified when your device is ready to be returned. Devices not collected or accepted back within 90 days after notice are considered abandoned and may be recycled or sold to recover costs. Reasonable attempts to reach you will be made first.

Contact form privacy

The details you submit are used only to respond to your inquiry. They're sent through a third-party form service to Precision PC's email and are never sold. Please don't include passwords or payment details.
