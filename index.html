How it works

You don't have to go anywhere. We pick your computer up and bring it back.

  1. Send a request

    Fill out the form and tell us what's going on. We'll get back to you within 24 hours.

  2. Pickup

    We'll set a time to grab it from your place or a public spot that works for you. Outside Franklin County? Just ask.

  3. Diagnosis and quote

    We figure out what's wrong for free, then send you a price. Nothing happens until you say yes.

  4. Return

    Most jobs take 1–3 days, longer if we need to order parts. We clean it, test it and bring it back.

About Precision PC

Precision PC is solely owned and operated by Ben Wheeler. He's been fixing computers since he was a kid and building them for people and local businesses since 2012.

We don't have a storefront, which keeps prices down, and you deal with the same person start to finish. Before you pay, you'll know exactly what was wrong and what we did about it.
