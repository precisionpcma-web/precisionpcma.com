How a build works

Nothing is ordered until you approve your build sheet.

  1. Answer a few questions

    About two minutes in the planner above.

  2. Get your build sheet

    Every part, its price and live price links, within 24 hours.

  3. Get the parts

    Order them yourself, or have us order them for you.

  4. We build and test it

    Then deliver it, stress-tested and ready to go.

Two ways to get the parts

Either way, you get the exact parts on your build sheet.

Order them yourself No fee

Buy each part in your name from the links on your build sheet. You own them from day one, and you pay us for labor when we deliver.

We order them for you + $25

We buy the exact parts at cost and hand you the receipts. Parts and fee are paid upfront by cash or Zelle, and labor at delivery.

Build questions

More answers on the FAQ page.

How do I pay?

Cash or Venmo, when you get your machine back. If we order the parts for a custom build, the parts and a $25 handling fee are paid upfront by cash or Zelle.

What's lifetime build support?

Every custom build includes free troubleshooting and diagnosis, help with manufacturer warranty claims (RMAs), a yearly dust-out, and driver and BIOS updates for as long as the original owner has the PC. Replacement parts aren't included unless the manufacturer's warranty covers them, and the travel fee still applies outside Greenfield.

Can I cancel a custom build?

Yes. If you ordered the parts, you own them, so you can cancel any time before we start building at no charge. If we ordered them for you, we return what we can and refund what the stores refund, minus restocking fees and return shipping. The $25 handling fee isn't refunded once parts are ordered.
