How a build works

Nothing is ordered until you approve your build sheet.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Answer a few quick questions in the build planner below. It takes about two minutes.

  2. Get your build sheet

    Within 24 hours you get a personal link with every part, its price and live price links.

  3. Get the parts

    Order them yourself, or have us order them for you. Your choice, right on the sheet.

  4. We build and test it

    Assembly, cable management, BIOS, Windows and drivers, then a full stress test before we deliver it.

Two ways to get the parts

Either way, you get the exact parts on your build sheet.

Order them yourself No fee

Use the links on your build sheet to buy each part in your name, shipped to you or to us. You own the parts from day one, and warranty claims stay in your name. You pay us for labor when we deliver the finished PC.

We order them for you + $25

We buy the exact parts at cost and hand you the receipts. Parts and the handling fee are paid upfront by cash or Zelle, and labor is paid at delivery.

Every build includes

Lifetime build support lasts as long as you own the PC. Replacement parts aren't included unless the manufacturer's warranty covers them.

  • Free build sheet with live price links
  • Clean cable management
  • Latest stable BIOS, XMP or EXPO enabled
  • Windows and drivers installed and activated with your license
  • Full stress test before delivery
  • Lifetime troubleshooting and diagnosis
  • Help with warranty claims (RMAs)
  • A free dust-out every year, plus driver and BIOS updates

Build questions

More answers on the FAQ page.

How do I pay?

Cash or Venmo, when you get your machine back. For custom builds, you either order the parts yourself, or we order them for you, with the parts and a $25 handling fee paid upfront by cash or Zelle.

What's lifetime build support?

Every custom build includes free troubleshooting and diagnosis, help with manufacturer warranty claims (RMAs), a yearly dust-out, and driver and BIOS updates for as long as you own the PC. Replacement parts aren't included unless the manufacturer's warranty covers them, and the travel fee still applies outside Greenfield.

Can I cancel a custom build?

Yes. If you ordered the parts, you own them, so you can cancel any time before we start building at no charge. If we ordered them for you, we return what we can and refund what the stores refund, minus restocking fees and return shipping. The $25 handling fee isn't refunded once parts are ordered.
