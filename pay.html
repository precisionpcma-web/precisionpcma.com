Paying for your service

You approve the price before we start. Then you pay in person, or through a secure invoice from Stripe.

Cash

In person, when you get your machine back or we deliver your build.

Card

Tap your card or phone on ours. Apple Pay and Google Pay work too. Payments run through Stripe.

Bank transfer

We text or email you a secure Stripe invoice. Transfers take a few business days to clear.

Paying an invoice

Invoices come from Stripe, our payment processor, with the Precision PC name and logo.

Open the link

It goes to a secure page on stripe.com. We never see your bank login or card number.

Get a receipt

Stripe emails you a receipt as soon as the payment goes through. It shows on your statement as PRECISION PC.

Lost it?

Text or email us at precisionpcma@gmail.com and we'll resend it.

Stay safe

Scammers copy small businesses. Here's what we will never do.

We never ask for gift cards, crypto, Venmo or Zelle

The only ways to pay us are cash, a card tap in person, or a Stripe invoice.

We never ask for your card number by text, email or phone

Cards are only tapped in person, or entered on Stripe's own page from an invoice we sent you.

Our payment links always go to stripe.com

If a link claiming to be from Precision PC goes anywhere else, don't pay it. Text or email us first.

Questions about a charge?

Contact us first and we'll sort it out quickly. You always keep your rights with your bank or card issuer.
